The Johnson family came to us with a typical suburban lawn—flat, thirsty, and providing little value beyond aesthetics. They wanted to grow their own food but didn't want a traditional vegetable garden that required constant maintenance.

## The Vision

Transform half an acre of lawn into a productive food forest that would:
- Provide year-round harvests with minimal maintenance
- Reduce water usage by 70%
- Create wildlife habitat
- Build soil health over time

## The Design

We designed a seven-layer food forest incorporating:

### Canopy Layer
- Apple and pear trees (dwarf varieties)
- Asian persimmon
- Mulberry

### Understory
- Blueberry hedges
- Currants and gooseberries
- Hazelnuts

### Herbaceous Layer
- Strawberries as groundcover
- Perennial herbs (oregano, thyme, mint)
- Comfrey for mulch production

### Root Layer
- Jerusalem artichokes
- Horseradish
- Garlic

## Results

After three years:
- **70% reduction** in water usage
- **200+ pounds** of fruit harvested annually
- **Thriving pollinator population**
- **Zero pesticide use**
- **Less than 2 hours/week** maintenance required

The Johnsons now enjoy fresh fruit from April through November and have become advocates for food forest design in their neighborhood!
