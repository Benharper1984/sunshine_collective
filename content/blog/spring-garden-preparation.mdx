As winter loosens its grip, it's time to start thinking about preparing your garden for the abundance ahead. Here's your complete guide to spring garden preparation.

## Assess Winter Damage

Walk through your garden and take note of any damage from winter storms, frost heaving, or animal activity. This is the time to:

- **Prune damaged branches** before new growth begins
- **Check for frost heaving** around perennials and press them back into the soil
- **Inspect structures** like trellises, raised beds, and fencing

## Soil Preparation

Healthy soil is the foundation of a thriving garden. Before planting:

1. **Wait for the soil to dry** - Working wet soil damages its structure
2. **Add compost** - A 2-3 inch layer worked into the top few inches
3. **Test your soil** - Know your pH and nutrient levels
4. **Consider cover crops** - If beds are empty, a quick cover crop adds fertility

## Plan Your Plantings

Now is the time to review last year's notes and plan this year's garden:

- **Rotate crops** to prevent disease and pest buildup
- **Start seeds indoors** for warm-season crops
- **Order seeds early** for best selection
- **Consider succession planting** for continuous harvests

## Tool Maintenance

Sharp, clean tools make gardening easier and healthier for plants:

- Sharpen pruners, hoes, and shovels
- Clean and oil wooden handles
- Check irrigation systems for damage
- Replace worn-out tools

## Early Season Tasks

Some crops love cool weather and can go in the ground as soon as the soil can be worked:

- Peas and favas
- Lettuce and greens
- Onions and garlic (if not planted in fall)
- Potatoes
- Brassica transplants

Happy gardening! 🌱
